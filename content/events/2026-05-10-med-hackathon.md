### Hackathon Theme: Smart Mediterranean

The goal of this year's hackathon was to design intelligent solutions for ecological protection, agriculture optimization, and smart city infrastructure tailored to the Mediterranean ecosystem.

### Track Highlights

- **Eco-Track**: AI systems for early wildfire detection and coastal water quality tracking using satellite imagery.
- **Health-Track**: NLP-driven clinical triage tools for rural community medical clinics.
- **Agri-Track**: Precision farming models optimizing crop irrigation based on local sensor arrays.

### Winning Project

Team *AeroGreen* won first place by designing a lightweight computer vision model deployed on micro-drones that detects olive tree diseases with 95.8% precision, running entirely on-device without internet dependencies.
